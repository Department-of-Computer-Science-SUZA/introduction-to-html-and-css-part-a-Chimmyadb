Coffee is a brewed drink with a distinct aroma and flavor, made from roasted coffee beans, the seeds of berries from certain Coffea species. Once ripe, the coffee berries are picked, processed, and dried. The seeds are then roasted to varying degrees, depending on the desired flavor. Coffee is one of the most popular beverages in the world and is enjoyed in countless forms, from espresso and cappuccino to cold brews and flavored lattes. Its caffeine content makes it a favored morning beverage, known for boosting alertness and enhancing focus.
